# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** December 17, 2024  
**Last Updated:** December 17, 2024

## Introduction

Picksy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our price tracking browser extension and related services (collectively, the "Service").

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information:**
- Email address (required for account creation)
- Password (encrypted and never stored in plain text)
- User preferences and settings
- Communication preferences

**Product Tracking Data:**
- URLs of products you choose to track
- Target prices you set for products
- Notification preferences for each product
- Product lists and organization preferences

### Information Collected Automatically

**Usage Information:**
- Extension usage patterns (anonymized)
- Feature usage statistics
- Error logs and crash reports
- Performance metrics

**Technical Information:**
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Extension version
- IP address (for security and rate limiting only)
- Device identifiers (anonymized)

### Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do not collect:
- Your general browsing history
- Personal financial information (credit cards, bank accounts)
- Social security numbers or government IDs
- Private messages or communications
- Location data beyond country-level for legal compliance
- Biometric data
- Health information

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses

**Service Provision:**
- Track prices for products you specify
- Send notifications about price changes
- Maintain your account and preferences
- Provide customer support

**Service Improvement:**
- Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- Identify and fix bugs
- Optimize performance
- Develop new features based on user needs

**Legal and Security:**
- Comply with legal obligations
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect user safety and security

### Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data based on:
- **Consent:** For marketing communications and optional features
- **Contract:** To provide the services you've requested
- **Legitimate Interest:** For service improvement and security
- **Legal Obligation:** To comply with applicable laws

## Data Security

### Security Measures

**Encryption:**
- All data encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
- Sensitive data encrypted at rest using AES-256
- Database encryption with rotating keys
- Secure key management practices

**Access Controls:**
- Multi-factor authentication for all staff
- Role-based access controls
- Regular access reviews and audits
- Principle of least privilege

**Infrastructure Security:**
- Regular security assessments and penetration testing
- Automated vulnerability scanning
- Secure development practices
- Incident response procedures

**Monitoring:**
- 24/7 security monitoring
- Automated threat detection
- Regular security log reviews
- Breach detection and response

### Data Breach Procedures

In the event of a data breach:
1. **Immediate Response:** Contain and assess the breach within 1 hour
2. **Investigation:** Determine scope and cause within 24 hours
3. **Notification:** Notify authorities within 72 hours if required by law
4. **User Communication:** Inform affected users promptly with clear information
5. **Remediation:** Implement fixes and additional security measures

## Your Privacy Rights

### Universal Rights

**Access:** Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
**Correction:** Update or correct inaccurate personal information
**Deletion:** Request deletion of your personal data
**Portability:** Export your data in a machine-readable format
**Objection:** Object to certain types of data processing

### GDPR Rights (EU Residents)

**Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate personal data
**Right to Erasure:** "Right to be forgotten" - delete personal data
**Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we process your data
**Right to Data Portability:** Receive data in a structured format
**Right to Object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests
**Rights Related to Automated Decision Making:** Protection from automated profiling

### CCPA Rights (California Residents)

**Right to Know:** What personal information we collect and how it's used
**Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information
**Right to Opt-Out:** Opt out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
**Right to Non-Discrimination:** Equal service regardless of privacy choices

### Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights:
- **Email:** privacy@picksy.com
- **In-App:** Settings > Privacy > Data Rights
- **Response Time:** We respond within 30 days
- **Verification:** We may need to verify your identity for security

## Data Retention

### Retention Periods

**Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
**Product Tracking Data:** Retained while products are actively tracked
**Price History:** Retained for 2 years after product untracked
**Usage Analytics:** Anonymized data retained for 1 year
**Security Logs:** Retained for 1 year for security purposes

### Deletion Process

**Account Deletion:**
1. **Immediate:** Account marked for deletion, access disabled
2. **30 Days:** Grace period for account recovery
3. **Complete Deletion:** All personal data permanently deleted
4. **Verification:** Deletion confirmation sent to registered email

**Automatic Deletion:**
- Inactive accounts (no login for 3 years) are automatically deleted
- Untracked products are deleted after 90 days
- Expired price history is automatically purged
