## Installation

### System Requirements
- Google Chrome 88+ or Firefox 85+
- Internet connection for price updates
- Notification permissions (optional but recommended)

### Installing the Extension

#### From Chrome Web Store (Recommended)
1. Visit the [Picksy Chrome Web Store page](https://chrome.google.com/webstore)
2. Click "Add to Chrome"
3. Click "Add extension" when prompted
4. Pin Picksy to your toolbar for easy access

#### Manual Installation (Development)
1. Download the extension package from GitHub
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/`
3. Enable "Developer mode" in the top right
4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the extension folder
5. Pin Picksy to your toolbar

### First-Time Setup
1. Click the Picksy icon in your browser toolbar
2. Create an account or sign in
3. Grant notification permissions when prompted
4. Complete the quick onboarding tour

## Getting Started

### Adding Your First Product
1. Navigate to any supported e-commerce site (Amazon, eBay, etc.)
2. Go to a product page you want to track
3. Click the Picksy extension icon
4. Click "Track This Product"
5. Set your target price (optional)
6. Click "Start Tracking"

### Supported Websites
Picksy works with major e-commerce platforms:
- Amazon (all regions)
- eBay
- Walmart
- Target
- Best Buy
- And many more...

## Using Picksy

### Product Detection
Picksy automatically detects products on supported sites:
- **Green icon**: Product detected and ready to track
- **Yellow icon**: Product detected but may need manual verification
- **Gray icon**: No product detected on current page

### Price Tracking
Once you add a product:
- Prices are checked every 6 hours automatically
- You'll receive notifications when prices drop
- Historical price data is saved for trend analysis

### Setting Target Prices
1. Open Picksy on any product page
2. Enter your desired price in the "Target Price" field
3. Click "Set Target"
4. You'll be notified when the price reaches or drops below your target

## Managing Your Products

### Viewing Your Tracked Products
1. Click the Picksy icon
2. Go to the "My Products" tab
3. View all your tracked items with current prices

### Product Actions
- **View History**: See price trends over time
- **Edit Target**: Change your target price
- **Pause Tracking**: Temporarily stop price checks
- **Remove**: Stop tracking and delete history

### Organizing Products
- Create custom lists (e.g., "Wishlist", "Urgent")
- Add tags for easy filtering
- Sort by price, date added, or discount percentage

## Notifications

### Notification Types
- **Price Drop**: When price decreases by your threshold
- **Target Reached**: When price hits your target
- **Stock Alert**: When out-of-stock items become available
- **Deal Alert**: When significant discounts are detected

### Notification Settings
1. Click Picksy icon → Settings
2. Go to "Notifications" tab
3. Configure:
   - Notification methods (browser, email)
   - Price drop threshold (percentage)
   - Frequency limits
   - Quiet hours

### Managing Notifications
- Click notifications to go directly to the product
- Snooze notifications for specific products
- View notification history in the app

## Settings

### Account Settings
- **Profile**: Update name, email, preferences
- **Privacy**: Control data sharing and retention
- **Security**: Change password, enable 2FA

### Tracking Settings
- **Check Frequency**: How often to check prices (1-24 hours)
- **Price Threshold**: Minimum discount to trigger alerts
- **Auto-tracking**: Automatically track viewed products

### Extension Settings
- **Badge**: Show price drop count on extension icon
- **Shortcuts**: Keyboard shortcuts for quick actions
- **Theme**: Light/dark mode preference

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Extension Not Working
1. Refresh the page and try again
2. Check if the site is supported
3. Disable other extensions temporarily
4. Restart your browser

#### No Price Updates
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify the product page still exists
3. Check if the site has changed its layout
4. Contact support if issue persists

#### Missing Notifications
1. Check browser notification permissions
2. Verify notification settings in Picksy
3. Check if notifications are blocked by system settings
4. Try re-enabling notifications

Picksy operates with strict legal compliance:
- **API-First Approach:** We use official retailer APIs whenever possible
- **Respectful Scraping:** When APIs aren't available, we follow robots.txt and rate limits
- **Terms of Service Compliance:** We respect all website terms and conditions
- **Attribution:** We properly identify ourselves to websites we access

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is Picksy free to use?**
A: Yes, Picksy offers a free tier with core price tracking functionality. Premium features are available for advanced users.

**Q: Which websites does Picksy support?**
A: Picksy supports major e-commerce platforms including Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, and many others. We're constantly adding new retailers.

**Q: How often are prices checked?**
A: Prices are checked every 6 hours by default. Premium users can set custom intervals from 1-24 hours.

**Q: Can I track products from multiple countries?**
A: Yes, Picksy supports international retailers and can track products across different regions.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why isn't a product being detected?**
A: This can happen if:
- The website isn't supported yet
- The page structure has changed recently
- You're on a search results page instead of a product page
- The product is out of stock or discontinued

**Q: I'm not receiving notifications. What should I do?**
A: Check that:
- Browser notifications are enabled for Picksy
- Your email address is correct in settings
- The product price has actually changed
- You haven't reached your notification limits

**Q: Can I export my tracked products?**
A: Yes, you can export your data from Settings > Privacy > Export Data.

### Privacy Questions

**Q: What data does Picksy collect?**
A: We only collect:
- Product URLs you choose to track
- Price history for those products
- Basic account information (email, preferences)
- Anonymous usage statistics

**Q: Do you store my browsing history?**
A: No, we only track products you explicitly choose to monitor.

**Q: Can I delete my account and data?**
A: Yes, you can delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Account > Delete Account.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: The extension isn't working after an update.**
A: Try:
1. Refreshing the page
2. Restarting your browser
3. Disabling and re-enabling the extension
4. Clearing browser cache and cookies

**Q: Price history shows incorrect data.**
A: This might happen if:
- The retailer changed their pricing structure
- There was a temporary pricing error
- The product was replaced with a different variant

Contact support@picksy.com if the issue persists.

## Tips for Best Results

### Maximizing Savings
1. Track products for at least 2 weeks to see price patterns
2. Set realistic target prices based on historical data
3. Enable multiple notification methods
4. Check competitor prices using our comparison feature

### Avoiding Common Mistakes
- Don't set target prices too low (you may never get notified)
- Keep your tracked list manageable (under 50 items)
- Update your email if you change it
- Don't ignore security notifications

### Advanced Features
- Use price history charts to identify best buying times
- Set up multiple targets for different urgency levels
- Use the API to integrate with other tools
- Export data for personal analysis
