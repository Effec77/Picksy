## 🐛 Known Issues

### Critical (None)
No critical bugs blocking release

### Minor Issues
1. **Puma Price Extraction**: Occasionally picks wrong price
   - Impact: Low (one site out of six)
   - Workaround: Generic extraction still works
   - Fix: Planned for AI version

2. **Service Worker Inactivity**: Goes inactive after 30s
   - Impact: Medium (Chrome limitation)
   - Workaround: Reload button provided
   - Fix: Not fixable (Manifest V3 behavior)

### Limitations (By Design)
- Only works on 6 supported sites
- Hardcoded selectors (breaks if sites change)
- Background scan takes 1-2 mins (rate limiting)
- Price history limited to 50 points
- No real-time comparison (manual links only)
